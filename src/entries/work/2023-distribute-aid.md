In 2023, I started building Distribute Aid’s open source and volunteer-based tech team. We started with our first cohort of Open Source Explorers, an outreach program that provides a well-structured introduction to open source while also making impactful contributions to facilitate Distribute Aid’s humanitarian aid work. Since then, I’ve further expanded our tech team and established processes that allow a team of volunteers, who can usually each only spend a few hours a week on our projects, to work together. My work involves both project and people management, finding the right people for the right jobs, and creating a big impact with few resources.
