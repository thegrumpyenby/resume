Hi, I’m Kai!

I’m a Jack-of-all-Trades working at the intersection of tech, marketing, and product. As a contractor, I predominantly work with dev tooling companies and help product teams explain what they’re working on. I communicate complex technical topics, and create clear communication for internal and external use. I devise content strategy and create relevant content that developers will love.

In my volunteer work, I am part engineering manager, part project manager, and part herder of devs. I happened to take to people management like a duck to water and truly enjoy that part of my job. Having such a varied combination of responsibilities is perfect for me .

I’m also a skilled interviewer thanks to my background in journalism and my endless curiosity and eagerness to learn new things. I enjoy public speaking, whether it’s giving talks, moderating panels, or interviewing people on livestreams and podcasts.
